# IdentiQ Documentation

IdentiQ is a digital identity and professional networking platform that brings professional profiles, digital business cards, contact sharing, lead generation, engagement analytics, and business tools together in one digital ecosystem.

## What is IdentiQ?

IdentiQ enables individuals and businesses to create and share a digital professional identity.

An IdentiQ profile can include professional information, contact details, social and professional links, portfolios, media, products, services, booking options, lead capture forms, payment options, and other interactive content.

Profiles can be shared through direct links, NFC, dynamic QR codes, digital wallets, and email signatures.

## NFC & QR Profile Sharing

IdentiQ supports physical and digital methods for sharing professional profiles.

### NFC

IdentiQ supports NFC-based profile sharing using compatible NFC cards and tags.

The documented NFC implementation uses 13.56 MHz NFC technology and NTAG216 hardware. A compatible device can access an IdentiQ profile by tapping the NFC card.

### Dynamic QR

IdentiQ supports dynamic QR codes that can direct users to an IdentiQ profile.

QR functionality includes:

* Custom colors
* Logo watermark
* Custom dot patterns
* PNG
* SVG
* Vector output
* Print-ready QR codes
* Dynamic destinations
* QR scan analytics
