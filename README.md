# IdentiQ Documentation

## Your Digital Identity. One Smart Profile.

IdentiQ is a digital identity and professional networking platform that helps individuals and businesses create and share a smart professional profile.

## What is IdentiQ?

IdentiQ brings essential professional information into one digital identity. Users can create a digital business card and share their profile using NFC and QR technology.

## Key Features

- Digital business cards
- NFC profile sharing
- QR profile sharing
- Professional portfolio
- Contact sharing
- Lead capture
- Profile analytics
- Professional links and information

## Who is IdentiQ For?

IdentiQ can be used by:

- Entrepreneurs
- Business owners
- Professionals
- Freelancers
- Creators
- Consultants
- Sales professionals
- Teams and organizations
