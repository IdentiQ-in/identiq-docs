About IdentiQ

IdentiQ is a digital identity and professional networking platform designed to bring professional information, contact sharing, engagement, lead generation and digital interactions together in one profile.

The IdentiQ ecosystem extends beyond a conventional digital business card. It includes digital professional profiles, NFC and dynamic QR sharing, contact exchange, lead capture, CRM capabilities, engagement analytics, wallet passes, email signatures, portfolios, consultations, storefronts, events and enterprise identity management.

NFC Sharing

IdentiQ supports NFC-based profile sharing through compatible NFC cards and tags.

The documented NFC implementation uses 13.56 MHz NFC technology and NTAG216 hardware. A physical NFC card can be paired with an IdentiQ profile and direct a compatible device to the profile.

NFC Characteristics

  • No mobile application is required for the recipient.
  • No battery is required in the NFC card.
  • Compatible modern iPhone and Android devices can access the profile.
  • The destination can remain dynamic.
  • Profile changes can be reflected without replacing the physical card.

Contact Exchange

IdentiQ profiles can be used to facilitate professional contact exchange between people.

The documented interaction flow is:

  1. Tap or scan an IdentiQ profile.
  2. Open the professional profile.
  3. Save contact information.
  4. Exchange contact details where applicable.
  5. Capture the interaction as a lead.

Contact information can be managed through the platform's contacts and CRM functionality.

IdentiQ Product Ecosystem

The public product architecture can be understood as multiple connected layers:

  1. Identity: Digital profiles, professional identity, verification, portfolios and custom branding.
  2. Physical Connection: NFC cards, NFC tags, QR cards, QR codes and physical display materials.
  3. Distribution: URLs, NFC, QR, Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, Samsung Wallet and email signatures.
  4. Engagement: Contact saving, social interactions, portfolio content, consultations, stores, events and payments.
  5. Lead Generation: Forms, contact exchange, CRM, notes, tags, CSV exports, webhooks and follow-up.
  6. Analytics: Views, taps, scans, clicks, saves, wallet downloads, devices, geography and campaigns.
  7. Monetization: Consultations, products, digital downloads, services, events and ticketing.
  8. Vertical Solutions: Professional users, creators, consultants, sales teams, enterprises, Realtors, brokerages, hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafes and events.
  9. Enterprise Infrastructure: Workspaces, RBAC, SSO, SCIM, brand controls, audit logs, domain controls, CRM integrations and data governance.
