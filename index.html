About IdentiQ

IdentiQ is a digital identity and professional networking platform that helps individuals and businesses create, manage and share a smart professional profile.

Digital Identity

An IdentiQ profile brings professional information together in one digital identity that can be shared with other people.

Digital Business Card

IdentiQ provides digital business cards that allow users to share professional contact and profile information digitally.

NFC & QR Sharing

IdentiQ profiles can be shared through NFC and QR technology, making it easy to exchange professional information.

Professional Portfolio

Users can present their professional information, portfolio and relevant links through their IdentiQ profile.

Lead Capture & Analytics

IdentiQ includes lead capture and profile analytics features designed to help users understand engagement and professional connections.
